Simple rule: record a payment once. The invoice, payable/receivable and linked buyer/seller record are updated together. UPI, Bank Transfer and Cheque require a reference number. Financial records are never hard-deleted; corrections use a reversal with a reason. Every completed payment also creates a controlled Accounting event for the existing maker-checker workflow.
