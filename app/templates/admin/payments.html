Simple rule: when a UFC reports that it paid AVPL, the amount stays outstanding until an AVPL authorized user confirms that the money was actually received. AVPL may also record a collection directly when AVPL itself has the receipt evidence. Goods/stock are never changed by payment confirmation. UPI, Bank Transfer and Cheque require a reference number.
